# Usefulness score review

These are the user-facing features whose verdict depends on their usefulness score rather than on the cost model: the low-usefulness, high-cost quadrant, the worst 20 under any hypothesis, the features that change quadrant in a sensitivity scenario, and the features one score step away from the quadrant line. Re-score them in `features.toml` (`usefulness`, optionally `reach`) and rerun `compute_metrics.py` and `sensitivity.py`.

Scale: 4 mission-critical, 3 high (large share of installs or strategically central), 2 medium (a segment, or experimental with a credible path), 1 low (niche, replaceable, or on a sunset path).
