Storybook Feature Audit

Every tracked source file in the monorepo is assigned to one of 101 features in 10 groups. Each feature's maintenance cost combines its code volume, its third-party dependencies (priced by release cadence and staleness) and its severity-weighted bug-fix and maintenance pull requests over twelve months. A usefulness score from 1 (low) to 4 (mission-critical), reviewed by the maintainers, and a reach estimate (share of installs) turn cost into cost-per-usefulness under four hypotheses.

Reading guide

  • Cost is a maintenance-effort index, not money: one source line is one point; a dependency costs 150 plus one point per release in the last year, plus 150 if it is stale or deprecated; a bug-fix PR costs 120 times its severity multiplier and a maintenance PR 40.
  • Usefulness is 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high, 4 mission-critical. The scale picker above changes how much more a higher level is worth: linear treats 4 as twice 2; the 3× scale treats each level as three times the one below (1/3/9/27); 2× and 4× are the same idea with gentler and steeper steps; reach divides by the share of installs that use the feature; inherited lets infrastructure borrow the score of whatever depends on it.
  • Cost ÷ usefulness ranks features by how expensive they are for what they give. Rank 1 is the worst ratio. The Decision map splits the same data into four quadrants; Sensitivity shows how much the order changes when one weight or modelling choice changes.
  • Click any feature, bar or dot for its full record: rationale, reach justification, dependencies, imports, pull requests and the paths it covers.
